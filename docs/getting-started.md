# Getting Started with TradieScribe

This guide will help you get up and running with TradieScribe quickly.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure you have:

- A TradieScribe account
- Access to the TradieScribe platform
- Basic understanding of your business requirements

## Step 1: Create Your Account

1. Visit [TradieScribe website](https://tradiescribe.com)
2. Click on "Sign Up" 
3. Fill in your business details
4. Verify your email address

## Step 2: Set Up Your Profile

1. Log in to your account
2. Navigate to Settings
3. Complete your business profile:
   - Business name
   - Contact information
   - Business address
   - ABN/ACN (if applicable)

## Step 3: Configure Your First Project

1. Click on "New Project"
2. Enter project details
3. Add team members (optional)
4. Set up project preferences

## Step 4: Start Using TradieScribe

Now you're ready to:

- Create quotes and invoices
- Track job progress
- Manage your schedule
- Communicate with clients

## Common Issues

### Can't log in?

Make sure you've verified your email address. Check your spam folder for the verification email.

### Need to reset your password?

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions.
