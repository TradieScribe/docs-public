# Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about TradieScribe.

## General Questions

### What is TradieScribe?

TradieScribe is a comprehensive business management platform designed specifically for tradespeople and contractors. It helps you manage projects, clients, quotes, invoices, scheduling, and more.

### Who is TradieScribe for?

TradieScribe is ideal for:
- Individual tradespeople
- Small to medium contracting businesses
- Construction companies
- Service providers in the trades industry

### What platforms does TradieScribe support?

TradieScribe is available on:
- Web browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- iOS (iPhone and iPad)
- Android phones and tablets

## Account & Billing

### How do I create an account?

Visit [tradiescribe.com](https://tradiescribe.com) and click "Sign Up". Follow the registration process to create your account.

### What plans are available?

We offer several plans:
- **Starter**: For solo tradespeople
- **Professional**: For small teams
- **Business**: For growing companies
- **Enterprise**: Custom solutions for large organizations

Visit our [pricing page](https://tradiescribe.com/pricing) for details.

### Can I try before I buy?

Yes! We offer a 14-day free trial with full access to all features. No credit card required.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

To cancel:
1. Log in to your account
2. Go to Settings → Billing
3. Click "Cancel Subscription"
4. Follow the confirmation steps

Your access continues until the end of your billing period.

## Features

### Can I create custom quotes?

Yes! TradieScribe provides flexible quote templates that you can customize with your branding, terms, and line items.

### Does it work offline?

The mobile apps support offline mode for essential features. Your data syncs automatically when you're back online.

### Can I integrate with accounting software?

Yes! TradieScribe integrates with:
- Xero
- MYOB
- QuickBooks
- And more

See our [Integrations Guide](guides/integrations.md) for details.

### How do I add team members?

Navigate to Settings → Team, then click "Add Team Member". You can control their access levels and permissions.

## Data & Security

### Is my data secure?

Yes! We use:
- 256-bit SSL encryption
- SOC 2 compliant data centers
- Regular security audits
- Automatic backups

### Who owns my data?

You own all your data. You can export it at any time and delete your account if needed.

### Where is data stored?

Data is stored in secure cloud servers in Australia (with options for other regions).

### What happens if I delete my account?

Your data is retained for 30 days after account deletion, then permanently removed. You can export your data before deletion.

## Technical

### What browsers are supported?

We support the latest versions of:
- Google Chrome
- Mozilla Firefox  
- Safari
- Microsoft Edge

### Why can't I log in?

Common solutions:
1. Verify you're using the correct email
2. Check your password (it's case-sensitive)
3. Clear your browser cache
4. Try resetting your password

Still having issues? Contact support.

### The app is running slowly. What can I do?

Try these steps:
1. Check your internet connection
2. Clear your browser cache
3. Close unnecessary browser tabs
4. Update to the latest app version
5. Restart your device

### How do I report a bug?

Report bugs:
- Via the in-app feedback tool
- Email: bugs@tradiescribe.com
- [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/TradieScribe/docs-public/issues)

Include:
- Steps to reproduce
- Expected vs actual behavior
- Screenshots if applicable
- Your browser/device info

## Mobile App

### How do I download the app?

- **iOS**: Search "TradieScribe" in the App Store
- **Android**: Search "TradieScribe" in Google Play Store

### Can I use the app without internet?

Yes! The app works offline for most features. Changes sync when you reconnect.

### My app isn't syncing. What should I do?

1. Check your internet connection
2. Force close and reopen the app
3. Check if there's an app update available
4. Log out and log back in
